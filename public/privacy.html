Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for CertStudy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Website: https://study.biagolini.click

1. Overview & Architecture

CertStudy ("we", "our", or "the application"), developed and maintained by Carlos Biagolini, is a client-side personal study companion for IT and cloud certification exams. Our application operates under a Bring Your Own Storage (BYOS) model. We do not maintain any central servers, backend, or external databases that store your study information, personal notes, or exam scores. This Privacy Policy describes what data CertStudy accesses, how it is used, stored, shared, protected, retained, and deleted.

2. Data We Access and Collect

Because CertStudy runs entirely in your web browser, all application state, notes, question banks, and exam history are created and stored by you on your own device. We do not collect, receive, or have visibility into this data on any server we control.

  • Local application data: Certifications, question banks, study notes, mock exam sessions, results, and preferences are saved in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Google user data (optional): Only if you explicitly connect a Google account, CertStudy accesses the specific Google Drive and Google Docs resources described in Section 3.
  • We do not collect analytics, tracking identifiers, advertising identifiers, or telemetry, and we do not use cookies for tracking.

3. Google User Data & Permissions

When you choose to connect Google Drive and Google Docs within CertStudy, the application requests the following specific OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Allows the application to create, view, and update only the files and folders created by CertStudy (specifically the /CertStudy folder and backup JSON files). It does not grant access to read or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents: Allows the application to generate and export your study notes directly to formatted documents in your personal Google Docs account.

4. How We Use Google User Data

CertStudy uses the accessed Google user data solely to provide and improve the user-facing features you request, specifically:

  • To back up and restore your study data to and from your own Google Drive.
  • To export your study notes as documents in your own Google Docs.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, credit assessment, or any purpose other than delivering these features. CertStudy does not use Google Workspace APIs or any Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

5. How We Store Google User Data

CertStudy does not store your Google user data on any server. Backup files and exported documents are written directly to your own Google Drive and Google Docs. The OAuth access token used to communicate with Google is held only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session and is not persisted to any external system controlled by us.

6. Data Protection & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All communication between CertStudy and Google APIs occurs over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • CertStudy requests the minimum OAuth scopes required (drive.file and documents) and never requests broad access to your Google account.
  • Because there is no central server or database, your data is not exposed to server-side breaches on our side; it remains under your control on your device and in your own Google account, which is protected by your Google Account security settings.

7. Third-Party Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

CertStudy does not sell, rent, trade, transfer, or disclose your personal data or Google user data to any third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers. CertStudy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

CertStudy does not retain your data on any server, because it has none. Data lifecycle is fully under your control:

  • Local data: Your study data remains in your browser's local storage until you clear it. You can delete it at any time by using CertStudy's data management options or by clearing your browser's site data for this application.
  • Google Drive and Docs data: Backup files and exported documents remain in your own Google Drive and Google Docs until you delete them. You can remove them at any time directly from your Google account.
  • Access revocation: You can revoke CertStudy's access to your Google account at any time, either in the CertStudy Settings panel by clicking Disconnect Google, or through your Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we change how the application uses Google user data, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
