Privacy Policy for CertStudy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Website: https://study.biagolini.click

1. Overview & Architecture

CertStudy ("we", "our", or "the application") is a client-side personal study companion for IT and cloud certification exams. Our application operates under a Bring Your Own Storage (BYOS) model. We do not maintain any central servers or external databases storing your study information, personal notes, or exam scores.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Because CertStudy runs locally in your web browser, all application state and question history remain strictly on your local device unless you explicitly choose to connect your Google account for cloud synchronization.

3. Google User Data & Permissions

When you choose to connect Google Drive and Google Docs within CertStudy, the application requests the following specific OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Allows the application to create, view, and update only the files and folders created by CertStudy (specifically the /CertStudy folder and backup JSON files). It does not grant access to read or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents: Allows the application to generate and export your study notes directly to formatted documents in your personal Google Docs account.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

CertStudy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never transfer or sell your Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.

5. Third-Party Sharing

CertStudy does not sell, rent, trade, or share user personal data with any third parties.

6. Data Deletion and Revocation

You can revoke CertStudy's access to your Google account at any time either directly in the CertStudy Settings panel by clicking Disconnect Google, or through your Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
